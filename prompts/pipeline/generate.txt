Write questions with each of these operators. Aim for 4-6 questions per operator.

- discriminate: a question whose answer would make one option clearly better than another.
- surface: a question that checks one of the assumptions.
- test: a question that checks whether a cause-and-effect link the decider relies on actually holds.
- bound: a question about the conditions under which an option stops working (scale, time, money, people).
- expand: a question that brings in a consideration or option the map does not cover yet.
- close: a question about the decision itself: what is hard to undo, what waiting costs, what deadline is real.
- calibrate: a question about what one of the decider's own estimates or impressions is based on.

Rules:
- Ask the decider, in plain words, one thing per question.
- Be specific to this decision. A question that would fit an unrelated decision word for word is useless.
- Do not build a claim into the question that the decision text does not support.
- Do not ask about stated facts.
- For each question give the id of the uncertainty or assumption it targets ("u1", "a2"), or "new" if none.
- For each question say how the decider could get the answer (whom to ask, what to look up, what to check).

Return only JSON in this form:
{"candidates": [{"operator": "discriminate", "target": "u1", "question": "...", "why": "...", "answer_path": "..."}]}
